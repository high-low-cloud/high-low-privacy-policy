Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

High / Low (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a family journaling app designed to help people share the best and hardest parts of their day and preserve family memories. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may handle, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Before publishing: Replace every bracketed item with accurate information and remove any data category or feature that your released app does not use. This policy must match the app’s actual code, hosting providers, analytics, and data practices.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, High / Low may process:

  • Account information: such as your name, email address, and sign-in details.
  • Family information: such as a family group name, invitations, and membership information.
  • Journal entries: daily “high” and “low” responses, dates, comments, and reactions.
  • Photos: images you choose to attach to entries.
  • Support information: information you include when contacting us.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and operate the app, create and manage accounts and family groups, display entries to authorized members of the same family group, store journal history and photos, respond to support requests, protect the service, and meet legal obligations.

3. Who Can See Your Information

Journal entries and photos are intended to be shared only with members of the family group to which they are posted. Do not post information you do not want other members of that group to see. We do not intend to make family entries publicly searchable or visible to the general public.

We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, such as account authentication, database, photo storage, hosting, and customer support providers. They may process information as needed to provide services to us and subject to applicable agreements.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a business transfer, where permitted by law.

4. Children's Privacy and Minimum Age

High / Low accounts are intended for people who are at least 13 years old. Children under 13 may not create an account or use account features. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete that information.

Users who are 13 or older but have not reached the age of majority where they live should use High / Low with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian where required by applicable law. Parents and guardians should help younger family members understand what information they share with their family group.

We will take reasonable steps to communicate and enforce the minimum age requirement. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us at highlowspprt@outlook.com.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain family journal history, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting highlowspprt@outlook.com. Some information may remain where retention is required or permitted by law, or in secure backups for a limited period.

Before release, ensure the app provides any account-deletion functionality required by the platforms on which it is distributed, and explain how family entries are handled when an account is deleted.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Access controls should restrict family content to authorized group members.

7. Your Choices

You can choose what to write and which photos to upload. Depending on the app’s available features, you may be able to edit or delete entries, manage family membership, and request account deletion. You may contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law.

8. International Users

Your information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, depending on where our service providers operate. Where required, we take steps designed to provide appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes or as required by law. We will update the effective date above and provide additional notice in the app or by other appropriate means when required.
